Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026 · Version 1.0
Global baseline: LISTIA is designed for users in multiple jurisdictions. This policy is intended to provide a common privacy framework while preserving mandatory rights available under applicable local privacy and data-protection law.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how LISTIA may collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal data when you use our websites, PWA, account services, AI features, integrations, property workflows, communications, billing and support channels.

2. Data we may collect

Account and profile data

Name, email address, authentication data, language, timezone, business information, organization membership, plan, preferences and related account settings.

Property and business content

Property descriptions, prices, locations, postal codes, commissions, PDFs, photographs, videos, price lists, messages and other materials you choose to provide to LISTIA.

Integration data

Data made available through services you explicitly connect, such as Google or other third-party platforms, subject to the permissions you grant. LISTIA aims to request access incrementally rather than requesting broad permissions unnecessarily.

Usage and device data

App events, feature usage, language and interface preferences, approximate technical location inferred from network information where allowed, browser/device details, security events, logs, crash information and performance data.

Billing data

Plan, subscription status, invoices, payment status, billing country, tax-related information where needed, Stripe customer/subscription references and usage records. Complete raw payment-card details are generally handled by Stripe or another payment provider rather than stored directly by LISTIA.

AI and communication data

Prompts, messages, files, transcripts, generated outputs and operational metadata necessary to provide AI, voice, automation, communication and support features.

3. Why we use personal data

  • Provide, personalize and maintain LISTIA.
  • Create accounts, authenticate users and manage organizations.
  • Process property materials and generate requested outputs.
  • Operate AI, automation, voice and communication features.
  • Connect services you authorize and synchronize relevant data.
  • Process subscriptions, Gestiones, invoices, taxes and payments.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, attacks and unauthorized access.
  • Measure reliability, improve product quality and troubleshoot failures.
  • Communicate service, security, billing and support information.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

4. Legal bases where required

Depending on your jurisdiction and the context, processing may rely on performance of a contract, your consent, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of vital interests, legitimate interests that do not override your rights, or another lawful basis recognized by applicable law. Where consent is legally required, you may withdraw it prospectively, subject to lawful processing already performed and any data we must retain.

5. AI providers and subprocessors

LISTIA may route certain requests to third-party AI, infrastructure, storage, communication, analytics or security providers. We aim to send only the information reasonably necessary to provide the requested function and to use contractual, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service. Providers may change as technology, pricing and availability change.

6. Google and other connected accounts

When you connect a third-party account, LISTIA receives only the data and permissions authorized through that provider's consent flow. You may disconnect integrations through LISTIA or the provider where available. Access tokens and secrets should be stored server-side and protected from exposure in the public client.

7. Voice and local processing

Where LISTIA uses device-local speech or browser capabilities, some voice synthesis or processing may occur directly on your device rather than on LISTIA servers. Availability and behavior may depend on your operating system, browser and installed language resources.

8. How we share data

We may disclose data to service providers and subprocessors that help operate LISTIA; to payment processors; to integrations you direct us to use; within your organization according to roles and permissions; in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition or restructuring subject to appropriate safeguards; and when required by law or necessary to protect users, rights, safety or service integrity.

LISTIA does not treat personal data as a commodity to be sold for unrelated third-party advertising purposes. If future activities trigger a statutory definition of “sale,” “sharing,” targeted advertising or similar regulated activity in a jurisdiction, LISTIA will provide the notices and opt-out mechanisms required by applicable law.

9. International data transfers

LISTIA is designed for global operation. Your data may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, LISTIA may use contractual clauses, adequacy mechanisms, recognized certifications, local hosting arrangements or other lawful transfer tools.

10. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining your account, providing the service, resolving disputes, preventing abuse, meeting tax/accounting obligations and complying with law. Retention periods may differ by data type and jurisdiction. Data may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion from active systems.

11. Security

LISTIA uses technical and organizational measures intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These may include encryption in transit, access controls, server-side token storage, authentication protections, network filtering, rate controls, logging and security monitoring. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, know, correct, update, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, obtain portability, opt out of certain uses, limit certain sensitive-data processing, receive information about disclosures, or complain to a regulator. Some jurisdictions also provide rights concerning automated decision-making or appeals from privacy-request decisions.

LISTIA will honor applicable rights subject to identity verification, legal exceptions and retention obligations. Authorized agents may submit requests where local law allows.

13. Children

LISTIA is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children below the age at which they can lawfully consent to the relevant digital service in their jurisdiction. If we learn that such data was collected without proper authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete or otherwise lawfully handle it.

14. Sensitive data

Please avoid uploading sensitive personal data unless it is genuinely required for a feature and you have a lawful basis to provide it. LISTIA may apply additional controls where law treats certain categories of data as sensitive or specially protected.

15. Cookies and local storage

LISTIA may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for authentication, language selection, security, session continuity, preferences, performance and other necessary functionality. Where non-essential tracking requires consent, LISTIA will implement jurisdiction-appropriate consent or opt-out mechanisms as those features are introduced.

16. Automated processing

LISTIA may use automation and AI to classify information, prioritize workflows, generate content, recommend actions or assist users. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, LISTIA is designed as an assistive system rather than an autonomous legal, financial, employment or credit decision-maker. Where a jurisdiction requires specific rights related to significant automated decisions, those rights will apply.

17. Business accounts and administrators

If your LISTIA account belongs to an organization, authorized administrators may manage membership, permissions, business settings and certain content associated with that workspace. Your organization may independently be a controller or business responsible for data it places into LISTIA.

18. Data-controller and processor roles

For account, billing, security and direct product-operation data, LISTIA may act as the party determining purposes and means of processing. For certain customer-provided business data processed strictly on behalf of an organization, LISTIA may act as a processor/service provider. Enterprise or regional agreements may define these roles more specifically.

19. Regional supplements

Additional regional notices may be introduced for jurisdictions with specific disclosure requirements, including parts of Europe, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, the United Arab Emirates and other markets. Those supplements will form part of this policy and will control only where required by applicable law.

20. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LISTIA evolves, adds providers or expands geographically. The current version and effective date will be posted here. Material changes may also be communicated in-product or through available contact information where reasonably required.

21. Privacy requests and contact

Privacy requests may be submitted through the support or account-contact channel available inside LISTIA. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Regional contact details, representatives or data-protection contacts may be added as LISTIA establishes legal entities or obligations in specific jurisdictions.

This Version 1.0 is an initial global privacy baseline and should be supplemented with jurisdiction-specific notices and professional legal review as LISTIA scales.
