Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026 · Version 1.4
Global baseline: LISTIA is designed for users in multiple jurisdictions and currently supports a multilingual interface including Spanish, English, French, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, German, UAE Arabic, Russian, Hebrew, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. This policy provides a common privacy framework while preserving mandatory rights available under applicable local privacy and data-protection law.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how LISTIA may collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal data when you use our websites, PWA, account services, AI features, integrations, property workflows, content creation, communications, multilingual and voice features, billing, Gestiones and support channels.

2. Data we may collect

Account and profile data

Name, email address, authentication data, language, timezone, business information, organization membership, plan, preferences and related account settings.

Property and business content

Property descriptions, prices, locations, postal codes, commissions, PDFs, photographs, videos, floorplans, price lists, brochures, logos, messages and other materials you upload or choose to make available through a connected source such as Google Drive.

Integration data

Data made available through services you explicitly connect, such as Google or other third-party platforms, subject to the permissions you grant. LISTIA aims to request access incrementally rather than requesting broad permissions unnecessarily.

Lead and communication data

Lead names and contact details; WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram, email and voice-channel identifiers; Telegram chat or business-connection identifiers where applicable; channel reachability; consent or lawful-basis records; consent-source and text-version metadata; opt-outs, blocks and suppression states; message status; property recommendations; delivery, read and reply events; call metadata; transcripts or recordings when enabled and lawful; and communication history needed to provide the service.

Usage, quote and billing data

Plan, subscription status, invoices, payment status, billing country, tax-related information where needed, Stripe customer/subscription references, Gestión type, requested quantity, displayed/authorized price or spending ceiling, quote status and expiry, actual usage, provider/service cost metadata used internally for routing/accounting, and related transaction references. Complete raw payment-card details are generally handled by Stripe or another payment provider rather than stored directly by LISTIA.

Usage and device data

App events, feature usage, language and interface preferences, selected device voice preference, approximate technical location inferred from network information where allowed, browser/device details, security events, logs, crash information and performance data.

AI, media and synthetic-content data

Prompts, messages, files, transcripts, generated outputs and operational metadata necessary to provide AI, voice, avatar, lip-sync, image, video, automation, communication and support features. If a user requests media involving a real person's face, voice or likeness, LISTIA may process that source content to perform the requested feature. Depending on jurisdiction and use, facial or voice information may receive additional legal protection; LISTIA does not use such material for unrelated identity profiling unless separately disclosed and lawfully authorized.

3. Why we use personal data

  • Provide, personalize and maintain LISTIA.
  • Create accounts, authenticate users and manage organizations.
  • Import, organize and process property materials.
  • Create or edit requested listings, images, video, voice, marketing and other content.
  • Operate AI, automation and quality-control features.
  • Connect services you authorize and synchronize relevant data.
  • Communicate with leads through channels the organization is authorized to use and track delivery, replies and attribution.
  • Respect consent, unsubscribe, suppression, do-not-contact and channel-preference states.
  • Calculate and present Gestión quotes, enforce spending approvals and select cost/quality-appropriate provider routes.
  • Process subscriptions, Gestiones, invoices, taxes and payments.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, attacks and unauthorized access.
  • Measure reliability, improve product quality and troubleshoot failures.
  • Communicate service, security, billing and support information.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

4. Legal bases where required

Depending on your jurisdiction and the context, processing may rely on performance of a contract, your consent, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of vital interests, legitimate interests that do not override your rights, or another lawful basis recognized by applicable law. Marketing communications may require separate consent or another lawful basis depending on channel and jurisdiction. Where consent is legally required, it may be withdrawn prospectively, subject to lawful processing already performed and data we must retain.

5. AI providers, communications providers, infrastructure providers and subprocessors

LISTIA may route certain requests to third-party AI, telecommunications, messaging, email, domain/website, infrastructure, storage, analytics, payment or security providers. Provider selection may change according to quality, cost, availability, privacy, region and product requirements. Internal routing may use destination or technical data needed to determine availability and price. We aim to send only the information reasonably necessary to provide the requested function and to use contractual, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service.

6. Google and other connected accounts

When you connect a third-party account, LISTIA receives only the data and permissions authorized through that provider's consent flow. Connected content may be indexed, analyzed, normalized or transformed to provide requested LISTIA workflows. You may disconnect integrations through LISTIA or the provider where available. Access tokens and secrets should be stored server-side and protected from exposure in the public client.

7. Communications, consent and suppression

LISTIA may maintain per-channel records indicating whether a lead is reachable and whether an organization has recorded the consent or lawful basis needed for a communication. A channel-specific unsubscribe or block suppresses that channel. Where a person's request reasonably communicates that they do not want further contact, LISTIA may apply a broader do-not-contact state rather than automatically attempting another channel.

Telegram reachability is stored separately from telephone reachability because a standard Telegram bot generally needs an existing user-initiated or otherwise authorized chat relationship. WhatsApp, SMS, email and voice providers may impose their own consent, registration, sender-identity, template and opt-out rules.

8. LISTIA Voice, recording and local processing

LISTIA can use text-to-speech voices supplied by the browser, operating system or device for its zero-additional-usage-cost voice experience. In that mode, LISTIA selects a compatible voice for the active language and may store the selected voice name and language preference locally on the device. The availability and implementation of SpeechSynthesis are controlled by the browser or operating system and can differ across devices.

Where LISTIA uses device-local speech or browser capabilities, LISTIA does not intentionally send the spoken text to a paid LISTIA voice API merely to perform that local playback. The browser or operating-system vendor may nevertheless implement its own speech service according to its terms and privacy practices. Cloud voice calls, call recordings, transcripts, generated/cloned voices or AI voice processing may be handled by approved providers when those separate features are enabled.

Recording, cloned-voice, automated-calling or synthetic-person features should be enabled only where the organization has the notices, consent or lawful basis required by the relevant jurisdiction.

9. How we share data

We may disclose data to service providers and subprocessors that help operate LISTIA; to payment processors; to messaging, email, telecommunications, domain/website and infrastructure providers used for authorized actions; to integrations you direct us to use; within your organization according to roles and permissions; in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition or restructuring subject to appropriate safeguards; and when required by law or necessary to protect users, rights, safety or service integrity.

When a person requests information about a property, development, or real-estate search, the information provided may be used to respond to the request, identify real-estate options that match the person's needs, and perform matching. For these purposes, the information may be shared with third parties or real-estate professionals who may offer relevant properties or options, and they may contact the person in connection with the person's real-estate search or inquiry, subject to applicable law, the scope of the request, and recorded communication preferences.

LISTIA does not treat personal data as a commodity to be sold for unrelated third-party advertising purposes. If future activities trigger a statutory definition of “sale,” “sharing,” targeted advertising or similar regulated activity in a jurisdiction, LISTIA will provide the notices and opt-out mechanisms required by applicable law.

10. International data transfers

LISTIA is designed for global operation. Your data may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, LISTIA may use contractual clauses, adequacy mechanisms, recognized certifications, local hosting arrangements, regional routing or other lawful transfer tools.

11. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining your account, providing the service, preserving communication consent/suppression evidence, preserving quote/payment/audit records where required, resolving disputes, preventing abuse, meeting tax/accounting obligations and complying with law. Retention periods may differ by data type and jurisdiction. Data may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion from active systems.

12. Security

LISTIA uses technical and organizational measures intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These may include encryption in transit, access controls, server-side token storage, authentication protections, network filtering, rate controls, logging, audit trails and security monitoring. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Your privacy and communication rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, know, correct, update, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, obtain portability, opt out of marketing or certain uses, limit certain sensitive-data processing, receive information about disclosures, or complain to a regulator. Some jurisdictions also provide rights concerning automated decision-making or appeals from privacy-request decisions.

LISTIA will honor applicable rights subject to identity verification, legal exceptions and retention obligations. Marketing opt-outs should be honored without requiring a privacy-rights request. Authorized agents may submit requests where local law allows.

14. Children

LISTIA is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal data from children below the age at which they can lawfully consent to the relevant digital service in their jurisdiction. If we learn that such data was collected without proper authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete or otherwise lawfully handle it.

15. Sensitive data

Please avoid uploading sensitive personal data unless it is genuinely required for a feature and you have a lawful basis to provide it. LISTIA may apply additional controls where law treats certain categories, including some biometric, voice or precise-location information, as sensitive or specially protected.

16. Cookies and local storage

LISTIA may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for authentication, language selection, locally selected voice preferences, security, session continuity, preferences, performance and other necessary functionality. Where non-essential tracking requires consent, LISTIA will implement jurisdiction-appropriate consent or opt-out mechanisms as those features are introduced.

17. Automated processing

LISTIA may use automation and AI to classify information, prioritize leads/workflows, select communication channels, recommend properties, generate content, route providers, estimate costs or assist users. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, LISTIA is designed as an assistive system rather than an autonomous legal, financial, employment or credit decision-maker. Where a jurisdiction requires specific rights related to significant automated decisions, those rights will apply.

18. Business accounts and administrators

If your LISTIA account belongs to an organization, authorized administrators may manage membership, permissions, business settings, connected content, communication settings, spending approvals/budgets and certain data associated with that workspace. Your organization may independently be a controller or business responsible for lead and property data it places into LISTIA and for determining whether it is authorized to contact a lead.

19. Data-controller and processor roles

For account, billing, security and direct product-operation data, LISTIA may act as the party determining purposes and means of processing. For customer-provided property, lead and communication data processed strictly on behalf of an organization, LISTIA may act as a processor/service provider. Enterprise or regional agreements may define these roles more specifically.

20. Regional supplements

Additional regional notices may be introduced for jurisdictions with specific disclosure requirements, including parts of Europe, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, the United Arab Emirates and other markets. Those supplements will form part of this policy and will control only where required by applicable law.

21. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as LISTIA evolves, adds languages, voice capabilities, providers, communication channels, pricing/authorization workflows or content-processing features, or expands geographically. The current version and effective date will be posted here. Material changes may also be communicated in-product or through available contact information and may require renewed consent where applicable law requires it.

22. Privacy requests and contact

Privacy requests may be submitted through the support or account-contact channel available inside LISTIA. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Regional contact details, representatives or data-protection contacts may be added as LISTIA establishes legal entities or obligations in specific jurisdictions.

Version 1.4 is a global privacy baseline and should be supplemented with jurisdiction-specific notices and professional legal review as LISTIA scales.
