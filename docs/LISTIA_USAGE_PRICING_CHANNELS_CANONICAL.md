# LISTIA Pricing & Channel Economics

Status: PRICING UNDECIDED — ANALYSIS REQUIRED BEFORE COMMERCIAL LAUNCH
Last reconciled: 2026-08-31
Currency: TBD.
Scope: LISTIA only.

## 1. Current decision
LISTIA does not currently have approved customer subscription prices, free/trial policy, seat pricing, plan limits, or usage markup percentages.

Do not display, quote, publish, sell, or hard-code plan prices or markup percentages until a dedicated pricing analysis is completed and approved.

## 2. Future pricing analysis
Before pricing is activated, perform a deep analysis that considers at minimum:
- customer segments and willingness to pay;
- value of qualified leads and marketplace exposure;
- included website/microsite value;
- number of properties and users by customer type;
- real infrastructure and provider costs;
- communications/media/AI usage economics;
- support and onboarding costs;
- competitive alternatives in Mexico and target international markets;
- gross-margin targets;
- churn/retention risk;
- seat economics;
- trial/no-trial/free-plan strategy;
- usage markup by plan;
- affiliate and white-label economics where applicable.

The output of that analysis will define the final three-plan structure, if three plans remain optimal.

## 3. Existing billing records
Legacy/test billing records may remain for technical continuity, but they are not an approved commercial price book. New commercial pricing must not be inferred from historical or test data.

## 4. Marketplace priority
Current product priority is Marketplace coverage, mapping, property/source/contact normalization, provenance, microsites, lead attribution, and Market Intelligence. Monetization is deliberately deferred until product coverage and operating data are stronger.

## 5. Usage economics principle
LISTIA must not intentionally execute billable provider usage at a loss. Exact customer markups and plan discounts remain TBD until the pricing analysis is approved.

## 6. Domain and media pricing
Domain markup, media generation markup, messaging markup, AI-provider markup and any other variable-cost pricing are all TBD. Existing supplier cost data may be retained internally for future analysis, but no customer-facing markup percentage is currently canonical.
