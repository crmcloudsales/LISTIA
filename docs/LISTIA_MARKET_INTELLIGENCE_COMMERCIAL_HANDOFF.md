# LISTIA Market Intelligence — Commercial Handoff

## What LISTIA can sell
LISTIA is not only a property marketplace. It is becoming a live observed-market intelligence layer built from its own continuously enriched inventory plus user behavior.

Commercial positioning for paid plans:
- See where competing supply is concentrated.
- Compare cities and property categories.
- Understand observed asking-price ranges and price/m2 by currency.
- See which bedroom mixes dominate the market.
- Track how inventory changes over time.
- Premium: compare observed supply against actual LISTIA engagement signals to identify segments that may deserve deeper development/investment analysis.

## Premium value proposition
Premium should be particularly relevant to developers, acquisition teams, investors, broker owners and commercial directors. The strongest promise is decision support: LISTIA can reveal what is abundant, what is scarce, where competition is concentrated and—when enough behavioral evidence exists—where user interest appears disproportionate to available inventory.

## What commercial messaging must NOT claim
Do not say:
- `LISTIA knows exactly what you should build.`
- `Low supply means high demand.`
- `This is 100% of the market` unless coverage has been verified for that geography and period.
- `Guaranteed investment opportunity.`

Prefer:
- `Observed market supply inside LISTIA's indexed inventory.`
- `Live market signals.`
- `Potential supply-demand gaps based on observed inventory and LISTIA user behavior.`
- `Decision support for development and acquisition teams.`

## Current Quintana Roo proof point — 2026-08-30
Current production baseline:
- 7,089 public published properties with a cover photo.
- 5,814 sale listings.
- 1,275 rental listings.
- 1,707 observed listings in Tulum.
- 1,509 in Benito Juárez.
- 1,049 in Playa del Carmen.
- 950 in Cancún.
- 132 in Bacalar.
- 71 in Isla Mujeres.
- 57 in Chetumal.
- 43 in Mahahual.
- 38 in Holbox.

Observed sale supply is led by apartments (2,790), houses (1,395) and land (1,009). Among listings with bedroom data, 2-bedroom product currently has the largest observed count (1,285), followed by 3-bedroom (1,117) and 1-bedroom (1,001). There are 1,718 sale listings without normalized bedroom data, so commercial materials must present bedroom charts with a completeness indicator.
