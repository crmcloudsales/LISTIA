# CloudCo Canonical Savings Policy

Status: ACTIVE
Version: 1.0
Effective: 2026-08-25
Scope: CloudCo, LISTIA, CloudSales, UpSales, white-label operations, internal agents, development tooling and customer-supporting infrastructure.

## Purpose
CloudCo continuously looks for legitimate ways to reduce development and operating costs while preserving or improving user value. Cost savings never override required quality, security, privacy, legal compliance, reliability, fidelity or mobile-first simplicity.

## Monitoring cadence
Review relevant provider opportunities at least every 2 days. Sources include provider email, official pricing pages, dashboards, newsletters, GitHub/open-source releases, free tiers, credits, grants, promotions, coupons, trials and price reductions.

## Decision rules
1. Permanently free / zero-obligation: if the benefit is genuinely free, creates no payment obligation or paid commitment, and passes license/commercial-use, privacy, security and legal checks, it may be claimed or used internally without additional owner approval when technically possible.
2. Free credits: may be claimed automatically when no purchase, paid commitment or hidden renewal obligation is created.
3. Free trials: may be activated without further approval only when upfront cost is zero AND cancellation before billing can be technically guaranteed and scheduled. Schedule cancellation at least 24 hours before first charge; prefer 48 hours when billing timezone or cutoff is uncertain. If safe cancellation cannot be guaranteed, notify the owner before activation.
4. Discounts / coupons / reduced-price plans / prepaid commitments / paid upgrades: always require explicit owner approval before purchase or acceptance, even when the discount is large.
5. Provider switching: cheaper/free providers may replace or supplement existing providers only when the required Quality Gate still passes and no meaningful security, privacy, legal, reliability or customer-experience regression is introduced.
6. Temporary free benefits are primarily internal savings. A temporary promotion must not automatically become a promised LISTIA/CloudSales/UpSales feature. A user-facing feature may rely on it only if the capability is sustainably free or economically viable, strategically useful, legally permitted and consistent with the canonical product definition.
7. User priority: prefer opportunities that lower customer costs, preserve low pricing, improve quality, increase reliability or allow CloudCo to provide more value without increasing customer complexity.
8. Audit: material opportunities and actions should be logged with provider, offer type, source, expiry, estimated savings, approval requirement, action taken and cancellation date where applicable.

## Scope examples
This policy covers AI models and APIs, Cloudflare/infrastructure, hosting, storage, domains, telecommunications, WhatsApp/SMS/email/voice, developer tools, GitHub tools, skills, agents, security products, analytics, design/media tools, databases, GPUs/compute, open-source software, SaaS trials, credits, grants and any future supplier relevant to CloudCo or its products.

## Mandatory safeguards
- No hidden paid commitment.
- No automatic purchase based only on a discount.
- No free tool is adopted without checking commercial-use/license terms.
- No free tool is adopted if it creates unacceptable data/privacy/security risk.
- No trial is allowed to roll into a paid charge unintentionally.
- No cost saving may lower the quality threshold required by the task.
- Provider identities and internal cost-routing remain internal unless disclosure is legally or operationally required.

## Authority
Later explicit owner decisions override this document and must be incorporated into the next version. This policy is a corporate operational rule and applies across products even when a specific product repository has not yet been created.
